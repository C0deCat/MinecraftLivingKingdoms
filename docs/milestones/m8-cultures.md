# M8 — Our own peoples, architecture and NPC looks

## Goal

Living Kingdoms' own cultures with recognizable architecture, clothing and way of life.

## Playable result

"Living Cultures": two distinct peoples with their own villages.

## Sources

- Building templates of our settlement mod; appearance and clothing via MCA.
- CTOV, Towns and Towers, Structory, Dungeons and Taverns — inspiration only. CTOV is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: modified structures may not be distributed. All culture assets are original.

## Phases

**A — foundation: a second people.** A second, stylistically distinct people on top of the working M4 pipeline.

**B — reconcile: culture, polity, settlement.** Separate `Culture` (language, architecture, traditions, goods), `Polity` (power) and `Settlement` (D-11). Fix inconsistent clothing, proportions and impossible layouts on complex terrain.

**C — integrate: culture content packs.** Compact culture content packs: architecture templates, work spots, construction stages, professions, clothing by status and age, cultural items.

**D — accept.** See [Acceptance](#acceptance) below.

## Acceptance

- Both cultures independently found, expand and maintain settlements.
- Buildings are usable for living and working.
- New peoples do not break the family and political model.
