# M1-D — accept: surface foundations, full validation, gameplay check

**Status:** Active

## Goal

M1 is accepted in singleplayer against the four bars of the [M1 acceptance](../../milestones/m1-world-and-adventure.md#acceptance), with the renderer and Complementary Reimagined enabled. Done means:
- the surface pieces that hang over slopes (D&T ruin town roads, WDA abandoned temple roads, illager campsite streets, Typhon, bathhouse bases) are grounded by our `foundation` processor, and the validator sees the fix (runtime passed, gameplay verified by the user for a sample);
- `npm run validate-world -- --full` reports 0 critical gaps on Small, Medium and Large × 4 seeds, with no ERROR/FATAL line that points at chunk generation (runtime passed);
- the same seed and size produce the same validation report twice (runtime passed);
- the user has gone through the progression checklist on a fresh Large world and taken spark readings with and without the shader pack (gameplay verified).
