# M1-D — accept: surface foundations, full validation, gameplay check

**Status:** Active

## Goal

M1 is accepted in singleplayer against the four bars of the [M1 acceptance](../../milestones/m1-world-and-adventure.md#acceptance), with the renderer and Complementary Reimagined enabled. Done means:
- the surface pieces that hang over slopes (D&T ruin town roads, WDA abandoned temple roads, illager campsite streets, Typhon, bathhouse bases) are grounded by our `foundation` processor, and the validator sees the fix (runtime passed, gameplay verified by the user for a sample);
- `npm run validate-world -- --full` reports 0 critical gaps on Small, Medium and Large × 4 seeds, with no ERROR/FATAL line that points at chunk generation (runtime passed);
- the same seed and size produce the same validation report twice (runtime passed);
- the user has gone through the progression checklist on a fresh Large world and taken spark readings with and without the shader pack (gameplay verified).

## Decision log

- 2026-11-10 — TD-13 (End gateways on a bounded End) closed as not significant, with the user: the validator finds End cities and Obsidilith arenas inside the End border on every size; gateways after the dragon are vanilla behaviour and are not checked separately.
- 2026-11-10 — TD-20 (teleports into fresh terrain stall the integrated server 2.4–9.5 s) closed as not significant, with the user: D-20 pre-generates every world at creation (TD-21), so a player never meets ungenerated chunks in the finished pack.
- 2026-11-10 — TD-22 split, with the user: surface pieces that hang over slopes are fixed in M1-D with the `foundation` processor; underground bunker hallways and rooms (covering them fills caves) and `graveyard:crypt` overlaps underground stay warnings and move to M7.
- 2026-11-10 — The scan is corrected before any processor is attached: `terrain_matching` roads and streets follow the ground block by block (the `foundation` processor skips them by design), and beards fill under rigid pieces of beard structures, so their warnings came from the scan, not the world. The processor is attached only to surface pools that still hang afterwards; the user's look at a ruin town, campsite and temple in step 5 confirms it in the client.
- 2026-11-10 — Memory (D-22, with the user, who plays with 8–12 GB): the pack's default and minimum is 8 GB; the test server gets 6 GB, 2 GB below the client, so validation runs under what a player at the minimum has. Independently, the `fixed_count` search at level load runs in its own pool of 4 threads instead of the common pool, so its peak memory no longer grows with the core count.
- 2026-11-10 — TD-15 (slow `/locate` of large WDA structures) does not block acceptance, with the user: moved to M11-A together with `/locate` reporting structures beyond the border; the validator does not use `/locate`.
- 2026-11-10 — The gameplay bar is a quick creative and teleport checklist by the user on a fresh Large world, not a survival run, with the user: presence and access are already checked by the validator on 12 worlds.
